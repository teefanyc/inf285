03Third conversation

Composite

Can't find the exact image needed, so pieces get combined instead — real elements, rearranged into something that didn't exist before.

What the AI touchesMultiple sources, combined

04Fourth conversation

Generation

No source image at all now — just a description. The designer needs a small icon asset, and asks for it outright.

What the AI touchesNothing pre-existing — text to image
Trust level — moderate to high

05Fifth conversation

Creative direction

The ask is no longer an asset. It's a strategy — a full set of branding directions. The designer stops making and starts choosing.

What the AI touchesThe concept itself
Trust level — high
The designer's request

“can you show me 3 different versions of branding concepts for an automotive parts & service marketing campaign?”

The open question

Where did the designer's hand go — and does it come back?

Each conversation hands the AI a little more of what used to be the designer's job alone. The visual language of this page follows that same curve: cooler and more contained at the start, bolder and eventually fully inverted by the end — creation giving way to curation.

What the lit review leaves open is whether that fade is a permanent rewiring of creative skill, or a temporary reallocation of it — attention moving from making to judging, rather than disappearing.
